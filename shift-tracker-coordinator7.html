Bulk import from Google Sheet

Your sheet just tells you AM, PM, or OFF for each date — set what those actually mean below, then paste.

Every AM code in your paste uses the AM option above, every PM code uses the PM option above. Use FULL for a whole-day shift (10 AM to 10 PM, fixed). On Thu & Fri, "close" automatically becomes 11:15 PM (and PM Long starts 2:15 PM) instead of the weekday 10:15 PM.
Paste the whole row as codes, space-separated — e.g. PM OFF AM FULL PM OFF OFF AM…. The 1st code lands on the "First day" date above, the 2nd on the next day, and so on. OFF days are skipped — they just show as free on your calendar. Rate is fixed at E£50/hr for every shift.
